Our Story

The Golden Fork Restaurant was founded in 2014 with a singular vision: to create a dining destination where exceptional cuisine meets warm Kenyan hospitality. What started as a small family restaurant in Karen has grown into one of Nairobi's most beloved fine dining establishments.

Our Executive Chef, trained in Paris and Cape Town, brings together the finest African ingredients with classical French techniques, resulting in dishes that surprise, delight, and linger in memory.

Every plate that leaves our kitchen carries our promise: fresh ingredients, crafted with passion, presented with pride — and served with the warmth that makes you feel at home.
